EXPLORE TOURIST PLACES IN KERALA

Kerala, known as "God's Own Country," offers diverse tourist experiences with its palm-fringed beaches like Kovalam, tranquil backwaters of Alleppey and Kumarakom best explored by houseboat, misty hill stations such as Munnar and Wayanad famous for tea plantations and wildlife, and rich cultural heritage found in places like Fort Kochi and the capital Thiruvananthapuram, making it a unique tropical paradise for ecotourism, adventure, and relaxation

The main 3 tourist spots are:

SREE PADMANABHASWAMY TEMPLE

The Padmanabhaswamy Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu in Thiruvananthapuram, the capital of the state of Kerala, India. It is one of the 108 Divya Desams that are considered among the most sacred abodes of Vishnu in the Sri Vaishnava tradition. Adi Shankara had composed sacred hymns on AnanthaPadmanabha and it is an important holy site for Smartha Tradition. The name of the city it stands on, Thiruvananthapuram, in Malayalam and Tamil translates to "The City of Ananta" (Ananta being a form of Vishnu).[1] The temple is built in an intricate fusion of the Kerala style and the Dravidian style of architecture, featuring high walls, and a 16th-century gopuram.[2][3] While as per some traditions the Ananthapura Temple in Kumbla in Kerala's Kasaragod district is considered as the original spiritual seat of the deity ("Mulasthanam"), architecturally to some extent, the temple is a replica of the Adikesava Perumal Temple in Thiruvattar in Kanyakumari district of Tamil Nadu.

MUNNAR

Munnar is among the most famous places to visit in Kerala. Surrounded by lush green hills, it’s a magical paradise tucked away in the hills of the Western Ghats at a towering altitude of 1,532 meters above sea level. The air is crisp and fragrant, carrying the aroma of freshly brewed tea leaves. It’s like a beautiful painting has come to life! There are several intriguing landscapes and Munnar tourist attractions to explore like its tea estates, Mattupetty Dam, and Eravikulam National Park, among others. Thick mist envelopes its hills, making everything look like a fairy tale! Waterfalls cascade down the slopes, their gentle murmurs harmonizing with the symphony of nature. Munnar casts a magical spell on people with its enchanting topography, making them feel happy and refreshed.
