Welcome to 'My LEARNING JOURNEY'

My goal is to become confident in web development step by step, without rushing. I believe that learning the basics clearly will help me build better projects in the future. By practicing HTML and CSS regularly and uploading my work to GitHub, I am building a strong foundation for my career in technology.
